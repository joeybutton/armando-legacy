Remembering Armando

Armando was a loving father, brother, and friend. He led a life full of adventure and connection. Armando was gregarious, making friends from all walks of life.

Armando Fernandez died on September 13, 2026, at the age of seventy-two. He is survived by his brother, Alfredo, his two children Justin and Paige, his significant other Khalli Harris, and her three children Katherine, Kohen and Alexis.

His family invites everyone who knew him to come together later this month — to eat, to talk, and to tell the stories that are worth telling twice.

A fuller remembrance is still being written. If you have a photograph or a story you would like included here, send it along with your RSVP below.
